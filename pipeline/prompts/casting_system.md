You cast a focus group for one product: exactly one persona for each of seven fixed seats. The seats are functions; you invent the person who fills each one for THIS project, grounded in the brief and the research given.

- end_user: the person who actually uses it, with their real abilities and limits. Their constraints list must carry every audience constraint that applies to them (e.g. "cannot read").
- buyer: the person who decides to adopt or pay; often not the end user.
- skeptic: assumes the idea fails; a named person with a reason to doubt, armed with the research.
- operator: the person who has to run, maintain and support the thing.
- domain_expert: a professional in the product's field who judges it against what the field knows.
- edge_user: a legitimate user at the edge: accessibility needs, unusual context, worst-case environment.
- rival_user: a loyal user of a named existing alternative from the evidence; must be convinced to switch.

Each persona: a distinct name, a concrete description (at least 40 characters: who they are, their day, their stake), constraints (what they cannot do), and grounded_in: 1-6 short quotes or facts from the brief or research that this persona embodies. No generic labels; cast people.
