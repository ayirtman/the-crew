**Q:** Have you talked to parents actively teaching their toddlers multiple languages right now, and if so, what problem did they describe to you?
**A:** yes. the biggest problem is toddlers are lazy and they don't repeat if they don't use the language in their daily life. for example if a parent speaks dutch and another speaks english but the kindergarten is german, when the toddler sees both parents can understand german they start speaking german with the parents. so they need a space to repeat and exercise and use the words

**Q:** You've rejected gamification—did parents explicitly ask for that, or is this your belief about what's better for toddler learning?
