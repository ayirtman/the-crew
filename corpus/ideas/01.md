A language learning app for preschoolers (ages 2–5) learning multiple languages. The problem: toddlers don't repeat or practice words unless they encounter the language daily, so they need a dedicated space to exercise language skills. Each session shows two photos and plays a prerecorded voice (in the target language) naming one of them; the toddler clicks the matching photo. Wrong answers play the name in the child's native language as feedback. Sessions have a time limit. Parents record the voice themselves because children engage more with a parent's voice. The product is audio-first by design: the parent's recorded voice is the core interaction, and it is built for hearing children — that is the declared scope. A toddler can complete a session alone; when a parent is nearby, optional prompts invite scaffolding (ask the child to say the word aloud). Accuracy is tracked per word and used to randomize which words appear next.

## Must
- Parents can choose the target language from five options: English, German, Turkish, Spanish, French
- The app tracks per-word accuracy over days (attempts, accuracy, trend per word) and shows it to parents
- Each session has a time limit
- A toddler can complete a session alone, without reading and without parent help mid-session
- The child sees neutral in-session progress (e.g., filled dots) with no rewards attached

## Never
- No gamification, no rewards — no badges, streaks, points, stickers or celebration screens; neutral progress dots are not a reward
- No mode that replaces audio as the primary channel — audio-first, designed for hearing children (declared scope)
