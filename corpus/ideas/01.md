A language learning app for preschoolers (ages 2–5) learning multiple languages. The problem: toddlers don't repeat or practice words unless they encounter the language daily, so they need a dedicated space to exercise language skills. Each session shows two photos and plays a prerecorded voice (in the target language) naming one of them; the toddler clicks the matching photo. Wrong answers play the name in the child's native language as feedback. Sessions have a time limit. Parents record the voice themselves because children engage more with a parent's voice. Accuracy is tracked and shown to parents, then used to randomize which words appear next.

## Must
- Parents can choose the target language from five options: English, German, Turkish, Spanish, French
- The app tracks and displays accuracy data to parents
- Each session has a time limit

## Never
- No gamification, no rewards—parents explicitly reject reward mechanics as a driver of technology addiction in young children
