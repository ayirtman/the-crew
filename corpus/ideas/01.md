a language learning app for toddlers. no gamification, no reward. toddler sees 2 photos and hears a voice - names of the of of the photos. i.e. dog vs cat and the voice says dog, toddler is expected to click on dog in the given language if the toddler selects the wrong photo then hears the name in his/her own language. the voice is prerecorded voice of his/her parents. the toddlers are expected to leard 2 languages at the same time, so the voice can be one of the selected languages. there should be a time limit of each session. the accuracy of the answers should be tracked for the parents and used for the randomization for the new words.

## Must
- the parent can choose the language to learn, any of the five (en, de, tr, es, fr)
- the accuracy of the answers is tracked and shown for the parents
- there is a time limit for each session

## Never
- no gamification, no rewards
